# What to wear (walking everywhere)

I walk everywhere, so I'm outside between buildings and on the way home. Use **feels-like temperature** (`apparent_temperature` in `data/weather.json`), not air temperature.

## Which hours to check
- Morning leaving home, midday between classes, and the **last time I'll be outside** (latest class/TA end or evening plan from `reference/schedule.md` and the calendar).
- November sunset is ~4:35 PM, so evening walks are colder and dark. Use the lowest feels-like from 4 PM onward when I'm out late (Tue until ~7:30 PM, Thu until ~8:30 PM).
- Say which part of the day is coldest and tell me to bring a layer if the evening is much colder than the morning.

## Starting thresholds (feels-like, °F; Neil to adjust)
| Feels like | Wear |
|---|---|
| ≤ 20 | Heavy coat, hat, gloves, scarf, warm layer underneath |
| 21–35 | Heavy coat, hat, gloves |
| 36–45 | Medium coat or jacket, sweater, hat optional |
| 46–55 | Sweater or light jacket |
| 56–65 | Long sleeves, light layer for the evening |
| 66–75 | T-shirt, light layer if out after dark |
| 76+ | Shorts and T-shirt |

## Add-ons
- Rain chance ≥ 40% or any rain: umbrella or rain jacket.
- Snow or ice: waterproof shoes.
- Wind ≥ 15 mph: windbreaker or an extra layer, even if feels-like is mild.
- Clear and sunny with a cold start: sunglasses.

## A glimpse into the future
- After today's answer, give 2–3 lines on the next few days (data covers 7 days).
- Call out changes that need action: a cold snap or warm-up (≥15°F swing), the first snow, rain on a day with long walks, or a very windy day.
- Tie it to the plan when useful (e.g. "Thursday's long evening class will be the coldest walk this week").
- Don't list every day if nothing changes.
